Questions? Answered.

The basics, in plain words.

Which files can I upload?

PDF, PPT and PPTX files, such as lecture slides and notes.

How do I know an answer is right?

Each answer links back to the page or slide it was built from, so you can check the source yourself.

Are quizzes graded automatically?

Multiple-choice and exact-answer questions are graded automatically. Short answers are compared against a reference answer and are marked as less reliable.

Does StudyMate replace my lectures?

No. It helps you review and practice what your course already teaches.
